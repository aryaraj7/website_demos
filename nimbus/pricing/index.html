FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Starter plan is free forever and the Pro trial requires no card — you only pay if you choose to upgrade.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time from your billing settings. Changes are prorated.

What counts as an event?

An event is any tracked user action — a page view, a button click, a signup. You only pay for what you use.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified with encryption in transit and at rest, plus role-based access control.
