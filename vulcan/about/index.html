Equipping Industry Since 1983

Started in a single warehouse in Pittsburgh. Four decades later, we outfit operations across 38 states.

Our story

Built by People Who Work

Vulcan was founded by a tradesman who was tired of waiting weeks for the right part. The idea was simple: stock the gear that industry actually needs, keep it in stock, and ship it fast.

Today that promise still drives us. Over 12,000 SKUs on the shelf, certified service crews nationwide, and a support line answered by engineers — not scripts. When your line is down, we move.
